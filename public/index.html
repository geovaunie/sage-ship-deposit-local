What link should I paste?

Open one of your successful SAGE transactions in Solscan and paste its transaction-page link. For example, a previous ship deposit. The app identifies a profile that your connected ship wallet currently controls; the old transaction grants no authority by itself.

Alternatively paste your public Player Profile address. In a decoded SAGE transaction this account is labeled Profile, not Sage Player Profile, Starbase Player or wallet. If the receipt is unavailable or has several matching profiles, use that address or ask official support to identify it.
